## Tidy references without memorizing rules

Every style has its own rules for initials, the position of the year, italics, and punctuation
between authors. This tool applies them automatically, so you only enter the source details.

## Supported sources

- **Journal articles**, with DOIs turned into https://doi.org links.
- **Books**, with edition, city, and publisher.
- **Book chapters** with editors.
- **Web pages**, with access dates.
- **Theses and dissertations** from university repositories.
