## Speak fluently on camera without memorising

A teleprompter scrolls your script slowly across the screen so you can read it while still looking
at the camera. It suits YouTube and TikTok content, lessons, live streams, online presentations,
and speeches.

## Tips

1. **Start slow.** Speed 4-6 fits a relaxed speaking pace; increase gradually if needed.
2. **Narrow the text.** A 50-70% width keeps eye movement small, so you don't look like you are
   reading.
3. **Write the way you speak.** Short sentences and blank lines between paragraphs give natural
   pauses to breathe.
4. **Rehearse once.** Read the script once with the teleprompter before recording.
