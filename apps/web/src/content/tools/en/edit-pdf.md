## Edit PDFs without paid software

Filling in a PDF form, adding a date, or placing a signature often means printing, writing by hand,
and scanning again. Edit PDF does it all in your browser, on a laptop or a phone.

- **Change text**: click an existing line, such as a name, date, or number, and type the new one.
- **New text**: fill in forms, add notes, dates, or numbers. Set font, size, color, bold, and
  italic.
- **White box**: cover text, numbers, or anything you don't want to show. Change the box color to
  match the page background.
- **Image / signature**: place a signature, stamp, or logo from PNG, JPG, or WebP.

## Tips for a clean result

Text changes work line by line: if a sentence gets longer, the next line does not move down like
in Word, so keep the length similar. The original font is replaced with the closest standard font,
so letter shapes may differ slightly.

Changed text and white boxes only hide what is visible. For confidential documents, the original
content underneath can still be found by someone who digs into the PDF file. Scanned PDFs or
photos have no clickable text; use a white box and new text instead.
