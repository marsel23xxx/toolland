## Four dimensions, 16 combinations

The test measures your preference on four dimensions, each with two poles:

| Dimension        | Left pole        | Right pole       |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Source of energy | Extraversion (E) | Introversion (I) |
| Taking in info   | Sensing (S)      | Intuition (N)    |
| Making decisions | Thinking (T)     | Feeling (F)      |
| Lifestyle        | Judging (J)      | Perceiving (P)   |

Your strongest letter on each dimension makes up your type code, such as **INFP** or **ESTJ**.

## Reading your result

The bars show your percentage toward each pole. Above 65% is a clear preference. Under 60% means
you are fairly balanced and may show both sides depending on the situation.

## Tips for an accurate result

1. **Think of what you do most often**, not one-off moments.
2. **Avoid Neutral** unless you are truly balanced.
3. **Answer for yourself**, not for your role at work or school.
