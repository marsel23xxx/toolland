## Four behavior styles

- **D (Dominance)**: firm, quick to act, and focused on results.
- **I (Influence)**: cheerful, persuasive, and sociable.
- **S (Steadiness)**: patient, loyal, and cooperative.
- **C (Conscientiousness)**: thorough, analytical, and quality-minded.

## Reading the chart

The center line is zero. A bar to the right means you often picked that style as most like you.
A bar to the left means you more often picked it as least like you. Your highest style is your
main style. If a second style is also above zero, the code has two letters, such as **DI** or
**SC**.

## Tips

1. **Picture one consistent setting**, such as work or school.
2. **Choose quickly.** The word that feels right first is usually the best fit.
3. **There are no wrong answers.** Every word describes a normal trait.
