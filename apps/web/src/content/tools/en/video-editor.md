## A light video editor with nothing to install

For everyday jobs like joining a few clips from your phone, cutting out the boring parts, adding a
title or captions, and putting music underneath, you do not need a heavy app. It all happens in
your browser, and the result is an MP4 ready for WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

## Save your progress with .ayu files

Not finished yet? Save the project as an **.ayu** file and open it again any time. Choose
**Light** if the original videos stay on the same device, or **With video** to continue on another
device. Toolland stores nothing on a server, so the .ayu file is the only copy of your project.
