## Five traits in one test

The Big Five does not put you into a single "type". Each trait is measured on a 0-100 scale, so
the result shows your own unique combination:

- **Openness (O)**: curiosity, imagination, and interest in new ideas.
- **Conscientiousness (C)**: order, discipline, and responsibility.
- **Extraversion (E)**: social energy and the drive to connect.
- **Agreeableness (A)**: warmth, empathy, and cooperation.
- **Neuroticism (N)**: sensitivity to stress and negative emotion.

## Reading your result

Scores under 40 are low, 40-60 moderate, and over 60 high. Read the note under each bar. The
"Your strengths" and "Room to grow" sections sum up the upside and tips for each trait.

## Tips for an accurate result

1. **Answer as you are now**, not as you would like to be.
2. **Do not overthink.** Your first answer is usually the most honest.
3. **Take it when relaxed**, not when rushed or upset.
