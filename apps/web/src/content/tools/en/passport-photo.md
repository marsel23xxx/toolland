## ID photos without a studio

Applications for jobs, schools, and government services often ask for a digital ID photo at a specific size. With a phone photo and a plain background you can make one yourself.

## Sizes at 300 DPI

| Size | Pixels |
| ---- | ------ |
| 2×3 cm | 236×354 |
| 3×4 cm | 354×472 |
| 4×6 cm | 472×709 |
