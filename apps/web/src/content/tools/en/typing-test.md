## Fast typing is a skill, not a talent

The average person types 35-45 WPM, yet with proper technique almost anyone can reach 60-80 WPM
within a few months. The difference comes down to finger placement, accuracy, and regular,
measurable practice. This test gives you that measure, with personal records so progress is
visible.

## Reading your results

- **WPM**: words per minute at the standard 5 characters per word; only correct words count.
- **Raw WPM**: every keystroke, including mistakes, as a measure of raw tempo.
- **Accuracy**: the share of correct keystrokes. Below 93%, the top grades lock out.
- **Consistency**: how steady your pace stays between seconds. The chart under your results shows
  where you slowed down and where typos happened (the crosses).
- **S-D grade**: a game-style blend of speed and accuracy. S takes 90+ WPM with near-perfect
  accuracy.

## Tips to raise your speed

1. **Accuracy before speed.** One mistake breaks your combo and forces a backspace; two keystrokes
   wasted on one letter.
2. **Use all ten fingers from the home row** (index fingers on F and J) and avoid looking at the
   keyboard.
3. **Short, regular practice** beats occasional long sessions: 10 minutes a day with a small
   target, say +2 WPM per week.
4. **Study your chart.** Valleys usually mean difficult words; frequently broken combos mean you
   are forcing the tempo.
