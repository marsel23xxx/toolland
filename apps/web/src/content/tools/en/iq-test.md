## Measuring reasoning, not memory

This test borrows formats common in nonverbal intelligence tests: Raven-style figure matrices,
number series, and odd-one-out puzzles. All three test your ability to infer a rule from examples,
often called fluid reasoning. Because every question is generated at random, you can retake the
test any time and still get fresh questions.

## Reading your result

- **Estimated IQ**: a score on a scale with mean 100. About two thirds of people fall between 85
  and 115.
- **Percentile**: the estimated share of people who score below you.
- **Scores by type and level**: show which kind of puzzle needs the most practice.
- **Explanations**: the key and rule for every question, so you learn the patterns.

## Tips

1. **Find one rule first.** In a matrix, go row by row: what changes and what stays the same
   (shape, number, fill, size)?
2. **Take differences in a series.** If they are not constant, look at the differences of the
   differences, or split odd and even terms.
3. **Don't get stuck.** Skip a question and come back using the numbers at the top.
