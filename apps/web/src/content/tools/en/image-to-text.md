## Copy text from images without retyping

Retyping a photographed document, a presentation slide, or a chat screenshot is tedious. This tool
reads the characters automatically (OCR, _optical character recognition_) so you only need to copy
the result. You can edit the text in the result box before copying it or downloading a .txt file.

## Tips for accurate results

1. **Shoot straight on, in good light.** Avoid shadows and glare on the paper.
2. **Keep the text sharp.** Move closer or use an original screenshot rather than a photo of a
   screen.
3. **Pick the right language.** Choose English only for English documents to improve accuracy.
4. **Check the words highlighted in yellow.** Those are the uncertain ones.
