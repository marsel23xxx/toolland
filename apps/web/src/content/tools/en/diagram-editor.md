## Draw diagrams right in your browser

Create algorithm flowcharts, business process maps, and technical diagrams without installing
anything. Shapes snap to a grid, connectors follow when you move shapes, and align and distribute
keep everything tidy.

## Key features

- **Fullscreen** and an infinite canvas with pan and zoom.
- **Smart connectors**: orthogonal, straight, or curved, with arrowhead options.
- **Format panel** for fill, border, line width, text, shadow, and opacity.
- **Undo/redo, copy-paste, duplicate, group**, and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Autosave**, open/save **.drawio**, and export **PNG** and **SVG**.
