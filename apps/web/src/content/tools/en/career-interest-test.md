## Six interest types

- **Realistic (R)**: likes working with hands, tools, machines, or nature.
- **Investigative (I)**: likes researching, analyzing, and solving problems.
- **Artistic (A)**: likes creating through drawing, writing, music, or design.
- **Social (S)**: likes teaching, helping, and caring for people.
- **Enterprising (E)**: likes leading, selling, and persuading.
- **Conventional (C)**: likes data, order, and clear procedures.

## Using your result

Look at your top three types. The best-fitting majors and careers usually combine your first and
second types. For example, **IA** suits architecture or product design, while **SE** suits
organizational psychology or HR management.

## Tips for an accurate result

1. **Choose by enjoyment**, not by skill or salary.
2. **Imagine doing it every week.** Would you still enjoy it?
3. **Answer for yourself**, not for your parents or friends.
