## Who is this template for?

For treasurers of residents associations, housing estates, clubs, community groups, and class funds
who want tidy, transparent records. See who has paid, how much is owed, and the cash balance in one
file, without ledgers or scrolling through chats.

## How it works

Members go once in the **Members** sheet. Each time someone pays, enter the amount in the right month
column of **Dues**. Other income and spending go in the **Cash Book**. Balance, arrears, and the
monthly report are calculated automatically.

## Tips for transparent books

- Keep a photo of every receipt and write its number in the Receipt no. column.
- Share the Monthly Report with members at the start of each month.
- Remind members in arrears politely; the largest arrears are listed on the Dashboard.
