## Who is this template for?

For small businesses, startups, clinics, shops, and offices in Indonesia that pay staff every month
but don't use payroll software yet. Fill in attendance and overtime, and BPJS, PPh 21, take-home
pay, and payslips are calculated for you.

## How it works

Fixed data such as base salary, allowances, PTKP status, and BPJS enrolment goes on the
**Employees** sheet once. Each month, add one row per employee on the **Payroll** sheet with days
present, sick, permission, leave, overtime hours, bonus, and other deductions. The template works
out overtime pay, BPJS contributions, PPh 21 (TER), take-home pay, and company cost.

## Tips for tidy payroll

- Update each employee's PTKP status at the start of the year (marriage or children change the TER
  category).
- Check the pension (JP) wage cap at the start of each year on the Tax & BPJS Rates sheet.
- Save each month's payslips as PDF for your records.
