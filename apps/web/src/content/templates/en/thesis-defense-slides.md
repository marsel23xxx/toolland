## How to build convincing thesis defense slides

- **One idea per slide**, with at most five short points.
- **Show numbers, not paragraphs**: key statistics read faster than explanations.
- **Keep tables short**: only the variables and values that matter.
- **Rehearse**: roughly one minute per slide.
- **Prepare backup slides** for likely questions.
