## Tips for a professional resume that stands out

- **Main column for what matters most**: profile, experience, and education.
- **Sidebar for quick facts**: contact details, skills, languages, and certificates.
- **Three to five bullets per job**, each starting with a verb and backed by numbers where possible.
- **Navy and charcoal** suit banking, law, and corporate roles; **green and terracotta** suit creative, education, and social work.

Applying through a job portal with screening software? Use the same details in the [ATS resume](/en/templates/ats-resume), and add a [cover letter](/en/templates/cover-letter).
