## How to write a leave letter

- **Send it as early as possible**, ideally before the working or school day starts.
- **State the dates and length clearly.**
- **Keep the reason short**: illness or a family matter is enough.
- **Mention attachments** such as a doctor’s note, and who will cover your work.
