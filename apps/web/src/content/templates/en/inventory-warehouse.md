## Who is this template for?

For shops, minimarts, online stores, restaurant kitchens, and small warehouses that want to know
**how much of each item is in stock, what it is worth, and when to reorder**, without buying
inventory software. Just record goods in and out; everything else is calculated for you.

## How it works

Every number comes from one **Movements** sheet. Each row records one event: opening stock, goods
received from a supplier, goods out (sold or used), a stock count adjustment, or a transfer between
warehouses. From there the template works out stock per item and per warehouse, average cost,
inventory value, gross profit, and monthly charts.

## Customize before downloading

Pick a business type (general warehouse, shop, food service, or online store) to get matching
warehouses, categories, and units, then edit them to suit you. Tick **sample data** to see 12 items
with a full year of movements before using your own data.

## Tips for accurate stock

- Record goods out every day instead of saving it all for month end.
- Set minimum stock to what you use while waiting for an order to arrive.
- Count your stock regularly, for example at the end of each month, and record differences as
  adjustments.
