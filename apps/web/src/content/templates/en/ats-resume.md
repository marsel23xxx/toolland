## How to write a resume that passes ATS screening

- **One column, top to bottom.** Sidebars and tables can scramble the reading order.
- **No photos, icons, charts, or skill bars.** ATS software can’t read images.
- **Standard headings** such as Work Experience, Education, and Skills.
- **Use the job ad’s keywords** word for word in your skills and bullet points.
- **Write achievements, not duties.** Start with a verb and add numbers.
- **Tailor it for each application**: adjust the summary, skill order, and most relevant bullets.
