## Who is this template for?

It is made for small business owners who want to know **whether they made a profit each month**
without learning accounting: shops, food stalls, service businesses, online stores, and home
businesses. Just record money in and money out, and everything else is calculated.

## Customize before you download

Pick a business type (retail, food, services, or online store) to get suitable categories, then
edit them as you like. Your business name, fiscal year, currency, and cash or bank accounts are
built into the file. Tick **sample data** to see a filled dashboard before using your own numbers.

## Tips for accurate reports

- Record transactions as soon as possible, at least at the end of each day.
- Keep business and personal money apart. Record money taken for personal use under its own
  category, such as _Owner drawings_, so it is easy to separate.
- Reconcile the account balances on the Dashboard with your cash and bank statements every month.
