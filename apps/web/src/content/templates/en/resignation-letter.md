## How to write a good resignation letter

- **Talk to your manager first**, then hand in the letter.
- **State the position and your last working day clearly.**
- **Say thank you** for the opportunities you had.
- **Offer a smooth handover** until your last day.
- **Keep it positive.** Leave complaints out of the letter.
