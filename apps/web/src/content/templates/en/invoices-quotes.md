## Who is this template for?

For freelancers, agencies, print shops, wholesalers, and service businesses that send invoices or
quotations. No more retyping numbers, calculating tax, or writing amounts in words: it is all
automatic, and you always know who still owes you.

## How it works

Each invoice or quote is one row in **Documents**, and its items go in **Line Items**. Totals, tax,
balance, and status are calculated. The **Print** sheet assembles everything into a ready-to-send
document with your letterhead, bank details, and signature.

## Collection tips

- Send the invoice the day the work is done.
- Check the **To collect** list on the Dashboard every week.
- Record every payment in the Paid column so receivables stay accurate.
