## Who is this template for?

For subject teachers in primary and secondary schools, and tutors who grade several classes at
once. No more working out averages, weights, and rankings by hand: enter the scores and the rest is
automatic.

## How it works

Students go in the **Students** sheet, whose rows line up with **Grades**. Enter assignment, quiz,
midterm, and final exam scores. The final grade uses the weights in **Settings**, then gets a letter,
pass status, class rank, and comment. The **Student Report** puts it all on one printable page.

## Grading tips

- Enter scores right after marking so the dashboard stays current.
- Check the **Need remedial work** list on the Dashboard to plan support early.
- Tailor the comments to the topics you taught this term.
