## Who is this template for?

For anyone who wants to know **where their money goes every month**: single professionals, young
families, students, and freelancers. Log your spending every day, then see whether your budget is
on track, how much you saved, and how close you are to your goals.

## Customize before downloading

Pick a profile to get matching categories with 50/30/20 groups and starting budgets. Change the
wallets, categories, and currency to suit you. Tick **sample data** to see the dashboard filled with
a year of transactions before using your own.

## Tips to keep the habit

- Log spending right away or before bed instead of waiting for the weekend.
- Pay your savings first at the start of the month, not from what is left at the end.
- Match the wallet balances on the Dashboard against your real balances every week.
