## Who is this template for?

For cafés, eateries, convenience stores, fashion shops, and small businesses without a point-of-sale
app. Record every sale, then see daily revenue and profit, your best sellers, and your busiest hours
to plan stock and staff.

## How it works

Each item sold is one row in **Sales**. Price and cost come from **Products**, so gross profit is
calculated straight away. Pick a month on the **Dashboard**: the daily summary, product summary, and
charts follow.

## Tips for a tidy register

- Match the Cash column in the Daily Summary with the cash drawer at closing.
- Enter product costs correctly so gross profit is accurate.
- Use the busy-hours chart to add staff or stock at peak times.
