## Who is this template for?

For homeowners planning a renovation, builders and contractors, small construction firms, and
civil engineering students. Work out what each job costs from material and labour prices, build a
tidy estimate, then track whether cost and schedule stay on plan.

## How it works

Material and labour prices go once in **Base Prices**. The **Unit Price Analysis** multiplies them by
the quantity needed per unit of work. In the **Bill of Quantities**, pick an analysis code and enter
the quantity: unit price, amount, weight, and summary fill in. Actual spending goes in **Actual
Costs**, and weekly progress shapes the **S-Curve**.

## Tips for estimating

- Take quantities from the drawings and add a 5-10% material allowance.
- Update base prices with your nearest supplier's prices before sending the estimate.
- Record every receipt and weekly wage so actual costs stay accurate.
