## Who is this template for?

For couples planning their own wedding, or working alongside a wedding planner. Vendors, deposits,
guests, and to-dos that usually live in chats and notes now sit in one file, with a countdown to
the big day.

## How it works

List items and vendors in **Budget**, deposits and payments in **Payments**, and invitations in
**Guests**. The **Checklist** comes with 25 planning steps whose deadlines follow your wedding date.
The **Dashboard** sums it all up: budget left, confirmed guests, and the next tasks and payments.

## Planning tips

- Keep 10% of the budget for surprises.
- Lock in the date and venue first; other vendors follow.
- Ask guests to confirm 2-3 weeks before for the final catering count.
