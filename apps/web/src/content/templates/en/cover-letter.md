## How to write a cover letter that gets read

A cover letter has one job: convince the reader that you fit the role, so your resume is worth opening.

- **Name the role and company exactly.** One letter per job.
- **Pick two or three strengths that match the job ad**, and back each with proof: numbers, projects, results.
- **Don’t repeat your resume.** Explain why you fit; the resume holds the details.
- **Keep it to one page** and proofread the company name before sending.
